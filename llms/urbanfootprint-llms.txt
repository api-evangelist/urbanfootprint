# UrbanFootprint

> UrbanFootprint is a resilient decision intelligence platform: urban, climate, and community-resilience data and analytics that help governments, utilities, financial institutions, and planners assess risk and make data-driven decisions. It is a closed SaaS platform (contact-sales) with no public developer API; the platform app authenticates via Auth0 and consumes an internal API at app-api.urbanfootprint.com.

Generated by API Evangelist from the provider's public surface on 2026-07-21 (urbanfootprint.com publishes no /llms.txt of its own).

## Platform

- [UrbanFootprint](https://urbanfootprint.com/): company site — decision intelligence for urban, climate, and community resilience
- [Analyst](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/analyst/): scenario planning and analysis application
- [Explorer](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/explorer/): map-based data exploration (public app at https://explorer.urbanfootprint.com)
- [Data Subscriptions](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/data-subscriptions/): curated urban / climate / hazard data feeds
- [Climate & Hazards](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/climate-and-hazards/): wildfire, flood, heat, and hazard risk data
- [Built Environment](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/built-environment/): parcel, land use, and built-form data
- [People & Vulnerabilities](https://urbanfootprint.com/platform/people-and-vulnerabilities/): demographic and social-vulnerability data
- [App login](https://app.urbanfootprint.com/): platform sign-in (Auth0 at login.urbanfootprint.com)
